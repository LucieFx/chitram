STUDIO VOLUME

दो रंग, एक कैनवास

Two souls, one easel, every hour of the creative day

Every artist knows that moment when the world outside falls silent. The studio lamp hums softly in the corner, paint tubes lie scattered across the teak table, and two brushes move across linen canvas without needing a single word to be spoken.

चित्रम् (Chitram) is built for that creative sanctuary. It is a live station designed to companion drawing artists, painters, and illustrators through the natural rhythm of creative hours. From first light sketches through midnight oil confessions, the music and mood change with the actual time of day.

The soundtrack is curated from acoustic indie hymns, timeless golden-era playback, gentle classical ragas, and soulful poetry — records crafted for focus, companionship, and authentic artistic expression.

Everything streams directly through YouTube's embedded architecture, ensuring original artists and composers are credited and supported. Every song links directly to its official recording.
